# Assessment 3: Reflective Portfolio — Task Details

## Why Reflective Practice Matters
The pace of technological change (Artificial Intelligence (AI), Internet of Things (IoT), and Blockchain, etc.) means that IT professionals must cultivate **change resilience**, underpinned by strong problem-solving abilities and the capacity to adapt quickly.

### How is change resilience achieved?
Reflection is a critical tool in change resilience, flexibility, and problem-solving skills. It enables practitioners to derive insights from experience, assess their responses, and plan for future improvement. This is particularly important because the nature of change remains fluid and unpredictable as people grapple with disruptive technologies like AI.

Reflective practice is also essential for effective teamwork and collaboration. It fosters empathy, mutual understanding, and the ability to navigate complex team dynamics. You will know the importance of these skills in supporting high-performance teams, having gone through the Week 1 and 2 content. We must reflect and question ourselves to work effectively together.

> 💡 **Consider this:** When was the last time you changed your mind? Success in IT often requires us to reassess our assumptions and adapt our approach. Reflection helps us step back, gain perspective, and move forward with clarity and purpose.

## What You Need to Do
In this individual assessment, you will prepare **three reflective journal entries**. 
* **Word Count:** 600–800 words per reflection entry.
* **Framework:** Every entry must strictly follow the **DIEP Model** for reflective writing.
* **Template:** Please use the unit's provided template to write each of your DIEP reflection entries.

### What is the DIEP Model for Reflective Writing?
The DIEP model is a structured approach used for reflective writing. It consists of four key steps, each corresponding to a paragraph in your reflection:

* **D - Describe:** In this section, you describe an insight—something new that you’ve learned or realised as part of the *IFN735 IT Industry Project Unit*. The insight should be relevant to your project and ideally have the potential to influence your thinking or behaviour in the future. You can relate the insight to your studies, professional practice, or future goals. 
  * *Example:* *"The most important thing I realised this week is the role of questioning in project management."* or *"I learned about efficient reading techniques from an exercise using a chapter from Anstey (2002) about the four resources model."*
* **I - Interpret:** Here, you interpret and evaluate the insight. Reflect on its significance, implications, and how it relates to your existing knowledge. Consider why it matters and how it might impact your future actions on your project or as an IT professional.
* **E - Evaluate:** Evaluate the insight further. What are its strengths and limitations? How does it align with other concepts or experiences? You may need to conduct some research to perform this evaluation adequately. Reflect on its value and relevance, especially within the context of your role as an IT practitioner.
* **P - Plan:** Plan how to transfer this new learning into practice or future learning. What steps can you take to apply this insight? How will it inform your future decisions or actions on your project or on future projects as an IT professional?

## Reflection Topics & Prompts

### Reflection 1: Mid-Stage (Up to Week 8)
Reflect on your initial engagement with the project and your early interactions with team members and stakeholders. Focus on experiences and insights gained up to the end of Week 8. 

* **Suggested Prompts:**
  * What contribution did you make that helped your team get started?
  * What challenges or opportunities emerged during the early planning or stakeholder engagement phase?
  * What did you learn about your role, communication, or expectations?
  * How do you grade your current performance (HD, D, C, P)?
* **Timeline Note:** Draft this part of the assignment by **Week 9** and discuss it with your tutor in Week 9 or 10. Formal submission is still **Week 11**.

### Reflection 2: Late-Stage (Weeks 9–11)
Reflect on your performance and team dynamics during the project. Focus on experiences and insights gained up to the end of Week 8.

* **Suggested Prompts:**
  * How did your role evolve as the project progressed?
  * What feedback did you receive, and how did it influence your actions?
  * How did you manage stakeholder expectations or respond to project changes?

### Reflection 3: AI Use (Throughout the Project)
Reflect on how you used AI during the project* and what you learned. 

* **Suggested Prompts:**
  * What you used AI for and why.
  * How AI helped or hindered your work.
  * How you verified AI outputs.
  * Any ethical or professional issues you had to consider.
  * How transparent you were to the industry partner about your use of AI.
  
*\* Note: This is separate from the "allowed use of AI" for writing assignments.*
